## Remaining source-review responsibility

The manuscript's author should check the primary passages on which they intend to rely, especially the philosophical books and chapter above. Metadata confirmation and reading an abstract are not substitutes for assessing an entire argument. The revision removes unsupported claims of historical priority, inevitable evolution, negligible oversight cost, consciousness proved by behavior, and immunity to collusion. It contains no invented empirical data, peer-review report, or affiliation.
